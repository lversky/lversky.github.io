01ABOUT

A little context

Built by curiosity.
Kept honest by people.

I’m a U.S.-based high school student, designer, writer, and programmer. I’m interested in systems: how a thing is put together, what it makes possible, and whether it works for the people using it.

I’m a FIRST Robotics Competition alumnus. On my team, I contributed to robot design and led sponsorship acquisition. I also served as a Student Senator and helped start student-led programs, including a Youth Poverty Support Council.

My current orbit includes generative AI and LLMs, embedded systems, and web platforms. I’m also concepting LzvelyHumanity, a nonprofit idea focused on human rights for people in the U.S.

↳

Designing high-tech projects since age five. The early prototypes involved sketches, breadboards, and a lot of LEDs.

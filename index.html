01About

A little context

Built by curiosity.
Kept honest by people.

I’m a U.S.-based high school student, designer, writer, and programmer. I’m interested in systems: how a thing is put together, what it makes possible, and whether it works for the people using it.

I’m a FIRST Robotics Competition alumnus. On my team, I contributed to robot design and led sponsorship acquisition. I also served as a Student Senator and helped start student-led programs, including a Youth Poverty Support Council.

My current orbit includes generative AI and LLMs, embedded systems, and web platforms. I’m also concepting LzvelyHumanity, a nonprofit idea focused on human rights for people in the U.S.

No hourly rate ever exceeds $80, and only $1,200 counts toward a single project, per person. If a project goes past $1,200, you do pay that extra — but it isn’t lost: the full amount paid over $1,200 becomes credit toward your next project.

Time reservation fee

Paid to reserve time for your project. Which one applies depends on whether the project counts as complex or ordinary — see the definitions below.

Ordinary projects$35

Basically everything else — anything that doesn’t meet the definition of complex below.

Complex projects$75

For example: full-stack apps, AI agent harnesses, etc., and the longer projects defined below.

What counts as complex?

  • Examples: full-stack apps, AI agent harnesses, etc.
  • More than 3 days, with more than 7 hours in total: code / designs needing more than 3 days, where all three days together amass more than 7 hours of designing — including breaks, with no more than 1 hour of those 7 hours being breaks.
  • More than 7 days: code / designs needing more than 7 days, where no more than 7 hours is amassed on each day — with breaks not included in that daily count.

Ordinary is basically everything else — if it isn’t complex by the definitions above, it’s ordinary and the $35 fee applies.

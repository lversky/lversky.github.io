About

Hey! I'm LzvelySky — a U.S.-based designer, writer, and programmer with a focus on building impactful, systems-driven work. I specialize in coding and ML.

I'm a FIRST Robotics Competition alumnus from a top-performing team, where I contributed to robot design and led sponsorship acquisition. I'm also a former Student Senator with experience initiating and scaling student-led programs, including founding a Youth Poverty Support Council aimed at driving tangible community impact.

These days my time goes to generative AI and LLMs, embedded systems, and web platforms — and to figuring out how much of my hardware budget can be justified with a "learning purpose."

Fun fact: I've been designing high-tech projects since age 5.
